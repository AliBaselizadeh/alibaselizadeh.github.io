ALIAS: RESEARCHER / SYSTEMS THINKER

THE TRAJECTORY

I trained first as an electrical engineer, then as a philosopher of science, and now study Human-Centered AI at the University of Milan. My work asks why the same measure or intervention behaves differently from one person to the next.

01Engineering taught me to think in signals, systems, control, and state estimation: where noise enters, how a trace is measured, and which patterns repeat.

02Philosophy of science made me ask what counts as a good explanation—and what a computational model can, and cannot, tell us about a mind.

03My current projects combine reproducible EEG and fMRI analysis, Bayesian computational modelling, evidence synthesis, consciousness research, and philosophical analysis.

04Alongside computational psychiatry and altered-state work, I am developing Distal sensitivity and the mark of the noncognitive: a philosophical project tested against engineered estimators, biological systems, and models of human behaviour.

COORDINATE / NOW

Developing open, testable research at the intersection of computational neuroscience, psychiatry, consciousness science, philosophy of mind, and philosophy of cognitive science.
