PHILOSOPHY OF COGNITIVE SCIENCE

Distal sensitivity and the mark of the noncognitive

A research essay separating causal, informational and normative readings of distal sensitivity and testing them against Kalman filters, internal-model controllers and bacterial memory.

PHILOSOPHY OF COGNITIVE NEUROSCIENCE

From contribution to necessity

An essay on motor signals, intentional binding and the sense of agency, examining the evidential difference between contribution, constitutive necessity and production claims.

PREDICTIVE PROCESSING / ETHNOGRAPHY

Whose Inference?

A critical review of predictive-processing accounts of spirit possession, using Zar rituals in Hormozgan as a test case and distinguishing private inference from the public inference of ritual specialists.

TRANSLATION

Computational Psychiatry: A Primer — Persian translation

A nearly complete Persian translation of Peggy Seriès’ MIT Press textbook, with publication rights under discussion through a Persian publisher.

EVIDENCE SYNTHESIS

Clinical and altered-state measurement

Ongoing systematic and scoping reviews on TMS–EEG perturbational complexity, sleep-related moderators of TMS plasticity, respiratory interoception, metacognition and bodily self-consciousness.
