02 — Research

Questions made testable

The work below combines open-data analysis, computational models, evidence synthesis, and philosophical critique. Some projects are clinical or consciousness-oriented; others ask foundational questions about cognition and explanation.

01

Resting-state EEG complexity in schizophrenia

Three-cohort replication and data-integrity audit using Lempel–Ziv complexity, permutation entropy, and the aperiodic spectral exponent across open datasets from Poland, Hungary and Iran.

02

Is the human explorer a Kalman filter?

Bayesian reanalysis of two open two-armed bandit experiments comparing Kalman-filter and delta-rule learners in PyMC, with PSIS-LOO, model recovery and parameter recovery.

Public repository →
03

Insula–prefrontal interactions during cognitive control

Open-data task-fMRI characterisation using Python and Nilearn; the paradigm was reconstructed directly from the data before planned GLM, ROI, connectivity and MVPA analyses.

04

Benchmarking mental-health text classification

A completed benchmark comparing TF–IDF and sentence-transformer representations across approximately 53,000 statements and seven dataset-derived labels, framed as a methods benchmark rather than a diagnostic system.

05

Distal sensitivity and the mark of the noncognitive

A philosophy of cognitive science project testing whether sensitivity to absent conditions can mark cognition, using Kalman filters, internal-model controllers, and bacterial memory in E. coli.

06

Whose inference?

A critical review of predictive-processing accounts of spirit possession, with Zar rituals in Hormozgan as a test case and a multi-agent simulation of public inference by ritual specialists.

with Prof. Maryam Gharasou, University of Tehran
07

Nociplastic pain and controllability

Two related projects with Amir Hakimjavadi: a computational layer for multimodal mechanism profiles in nociplastic pain, and a reanalysis of controllability, precision and block structure using released code and data.

08

TMS–EEG complexity and altered states

Evidence synthesis mapping perturbational complexity across disorders of consciousness, schizophrenia, ego dissolution and Alzheimer’s disease, alongside reviews of respiratory interoception and TMS-induced plasticity.
