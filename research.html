02 — Research

Four active projects

01

Circuit versus Diagnosis

A systematic review and meta-regression testing whether the site of stimulation, rather than diagnostic category, better predicts the cognitive effects of tDCS — working memory, response inhibition, and cognitive flexibility — across psychiatric disorders.

02

Complexity as a Marker of Consciousness

A scoping review mapping the Perturbational Complexity Index and related TMS-EEG complexity measures across disorders of consciousness, schizophrenia, ego dissolution, and Alzheimer's disease.

03

Resting-State EEG Complexity

Testing whether complexity biomarkers — Lempel-Ziv complexity, permutation entropy, and the spectral exponent — generalize across two open EEG datasets, implemented in MNE-Python with FOOOF.

04

Breath, Belief, and the Zar Ritual

A theoretical paper framing respiratory interoception and precision re-weighting as a computational account of Zar healing rituals, with spirit possession treated as a cultural prior.

with Prof. Maryam Gharasou, ethnomusicology, University of Tehran
