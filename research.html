02RESEARCHROUTE / 02

Questions made testable.

Open-data analyses, computational models, evidence audits, and philosophical critique connected by one concern: what must a measurement show before it supports a claim about mind or mental health?

CURRENT WORK

These projects move from reproducible analysis to computational explanation and philosophical critique. Public repositories are linked where the work is available for inspection.

01

OPEN DATA / MNE-PYTHON / REPLICATION

Resting-State EEG Complexity

A three-cohort replication test of Lempel–Ziv complexity, permutation entropy, and the aperiodic spectral exponent in schizophrenia, paired with a data-integrity audit.

INDEPENDENT
02

BAYESIAN MODELLING / OPEN DATA

Is the Human Explorer a Kalman Filter?

A reanalysis of uncertainty-driven exploration data comparing Kalman-filter and delta-rule learners with hierarchical Bayesian fits, PSIS-LOO, model recovery, and parameter recovery.

Open repository ↗
03

TASK-FMRI / NILEARN / REPRODUCIBILITY

Insula–Prefrontal Interactions

An open-data task-fMRI workflow that reconstructs the experimental paradigm from the data itself before GLM, ROI, connectivity, and decoding analyses.

04

NLP / CLINICAL LANGUAGE / AUDIT

Benchmarking Mental-Health Text Classification

A benchmark comparing TF–IDF and sentence embeddings across approximately 53,000 statements and seven dataset-derived labels.

Open repository ↗
05

PHILOSOPHY / CONTROL THEORY / STATE ESTIMATION

Distal Sensitivity and the Mark of the Noncognitive

A philosophy of cognitive science project testing whether sensitivity to absent conditions can mark cognition, using Kalman filters, internal-model controllers, bacterial memory, and behavioural models.

06

PREDICTIVE PROCESSING / SIMULATION / PHILOSOPHY

Whose Inference?

A critical review of predictive-processing accounts of spirit possession, with Zar rituals in Hormozgan as a test case and a multi-agent simulation.

Open repository ↗
07

MULTIMODAL AI / REANALYSIS / PRECISION

Nociplastic Pain and Controllability

Two related projects with Amir Hakimjavadi: computational mechanism profiles for multimodal AI in nociplastic pain, and a reanalysis of controllability, precision, and block structure.

08

EVIDENCE SYNTHESIS / CONSCIOUSNESS

TMS–EEG Complexity Across Clinical and Altered States

A scoping review mapping perturbational complexity across disorders of consciousness, schizophrenia, ego dissolution, and Alzheimer’s disease, alongside reviews of respiratory interoception and TMS-induced plasticity.
